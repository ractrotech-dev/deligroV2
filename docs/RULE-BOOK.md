# Deligro rule book

How the business works, as decided by the owner. The website, admin panel and all
four apps must follow these rules. If code disagrees with this page, the code is
wrong. Add new rules here as the owner decides them.

## Money

- **Deligro receives all customer money.** The rider takes no money from the shop at
  pickup. The customer pays Deligro: cash to the rider, or online once online payment
  is switched on.
- **Deligro pays the shop** on the shop's weekly or monthly settlement.
- **What a shop gets for each order:** customer paid, less delivery, tax and tip, less
  Deligro's cut (commission, GST on commission, other charges).
- **The owner alone can approve payments** to shops and riders. The app records who
  approved and why. Managers keep the permissions they are given.
- **Riders get a fixed salary only,** paid outside the app and recorded in it. No
  incentives, bonuses, targets or extra pay, and riders don't see those features.

## Customer wallet

For when a rider has no change, for example an order of ₹150 paid with a ₹200 note.

- **Adding:** the rider adds the ₹50 to the customer's wallet as points. 1 point = ₹1.
- **Proof:** the customer's app shows a code and the customer tells the rider. The rider
  types it in. Without the right code, no points are added.
- **Limit:** the admin panel sets the most a rider can add on one order.
- **Using:** at checkout the customer chooses to use the wallet, for any amount. Points
  can cover part of an order or all of it. If they cover all of it, the rider collects no
  cash.
- **Points never expire** and can't be taken out as cash. Only an admin can correct a
  balance, with a written reason.
- **The money:** the rider hands over all the cash received, ₹200 in this example. Deligro
  holds the ₹50 for the customer.
- **Shops are not affected:** when a customer pays with points, Deligro still pays the shop
  its normal amount.
- Every point added, used or corrected is recorded with who, when and which order.

## Adding vendors

- **Only the admin panel and managers can add a vendor.** Vendors and customers cannot
  create a vendor account or sign themselves up.
- **There is no review or approval step.** A shop is live as soon as it is added.
- **A shop needs a map pin to go live.** Without one it stays off until someone pins it
  and switches it on, because the app cannot check its delivery area.
- Vendor documents are kept on file. Nobody verifies or rejects them.

## Controlling features

The admin panel decides which features customers, shops, riders and managers can use,
for everyone or for one person or shop.

## Payment methods

- **Cash only for now.** Razorpay online payment comes later.
- **The admin panel controls this:** whether online payment is on, and the cash limit.
  Above the cash limit, the order can only be paid online.

## Rider cash

- Cash can go from the rider to a manager and then to the owner, or from the rider
  directly to the owner.
- A manager or the owner confirms they received it. One confirmation is enough.
- The app always records who confirmed it.

## Cancelling

- **Customer:** can cancel only within 20 seconds of placing the order. After that the
  order will be delivered.
- **Shop:** cannot cancel.
- **Admin and managers:** can cancel at any time, with a written reason. The app records
  who did it.

## When an order can't be delivered

If the shop rejects it, the shop is closed, or there is no rider, a manager or admin
contacts the customer and decides each time.

## Refunds and complaints

- **No refunds for customers or vendors.** Refund screens and buttons are removed from
  every app and from the panel. Old refund records stay in the database and in reports.
- **Complaints after delivery** (cold food, missing item, wrong order): support talks to
  the customer and records it. No compensation is given.

## Self-pickup

- A customer can choose to collect the order from the shop.
- The customer pays Deligro, then collects. The shop does not take the money.
